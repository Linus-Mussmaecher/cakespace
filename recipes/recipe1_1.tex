\section*{Orange Upside Down Cake}

\subsection*{Orange slice base/topping}
\begin{itemize}
 \item 2 large oranges
 \item 2 tablespoon olive oil
 \item 50g sugar
\end{itemize}

\subsection*{Batter}
 \begin{itemize}
 \item 180 ml plant-based milk
 \item 120 ml olive oil
 \item 100 g sugar (some of it may be vanilla sugar or add some vanilla extract)
 \item 2 tablespoon orange zest
 \item 350 g all-purpose flour
 \item juice of 1 lemon
 \item 1 tablespoon baking powder
 \item 1 tablespoon orange water
 \item 1/2 teaspoon salt
\end{itemize}

\subsection*{Instructions}
\begin{enumerate}
 \item Preheat your oven to 180ºC. Line the bottom of a cake pan with parchment paper.
 \item Slice the oranges into 5 mm rounds, then remove the skin.
 \item Mix 2 tablespoon of olive oil with 50 g of sugar and pour it into the bottom of the cake pan. Arrange the orange slices on top of the sugar mixture in the pan. Set aside.
 \item Mix wet ingredients: In a bowl, whisk together the plant-based milk, lemon juice and orange water. Let it sit for 5 minutes, then add the olive oil and mix again.
 \item In another bowl, add the sugar and orange zest. Rub the sugar and zest together to release the natural oils, enhancing the orange flavor.
 \item Gradually add the wet ingredients to the sugar-zest mixture, stirring until smooth.
 \item In a separate large bowl, combine the all-purpose flour, salt and baking powder.
 \item Pour the wet mixture into the dry ingredients, stirring gently until just combined. Be careful not to overmix.
 \item Pour the batter into the prepared cake pan, smoothing the top with a spatula. Bake for approximately 43-45 minutes, or until a toothpick inserted into the center comes out clean.
 \item Once baked, remove the cake from the oven and allow it to cool completely. When ready to serve, carefully flip the cake upside down onto a large plate.
\end{enumerate}
